SnapCrop brings the iOS "Crop, Copy & Delete" screenshot flow to Android and GrapheneOS.

Take a screenshot, tap Edit and pick SnapCrop. Drag the corners to crop, then tap <b>Copy & Delete</b>. The cropped image goes straight to your clipboard, ready to paste into any chat or notes app, and the original screenshot is removed so it never clutters your gallery.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Copy & Delete: crop, copy to the clipboard and remove the original in one tap</li>
<li>Save or Delete: keep the cropped version, or throw away an accidental screenshot</li>
<li>Nothing runs in the background. SnapCrop only opens when you edit a screenshot</li>
<li>Disappears from Recent Apps as soon as you are done</li>
<li>Fully offline. SnapCrop has no internet access</li>
<li>Works with GrapheneOS Storage Scopes, so you can limit it to your Screenshots folder</li>
</ul>
