Add to F-Droid

Tap this on your phone with F-Droid installed.

On another device? Scan this with your phone's camera or F-Droid's scanner.
Adding it by hand
  1. In F-Droid, open Settings → Repositories and tap +.
  2. Enter this address:
    @REPO_URL@
  3. Check that F-Droid shows this fingerprint:
    @FINGERPRINT@
